# Tactical infantry and squad Move at Will on Steam build 26734796

**Host-only local prototype.** On the matching executable, the DLL enables all 61 hooks, including the 15 tactical infantry hooks. The prototype explicitly opts in at startup. The implementation and manifest retain `runtime_allowed: false` pending live smoke verification. The executable gate is the game PE SHA-256 `81a13de787c1869b766004cdcadb9ff99012f0b1b58b89db9a7741b10b9f059a`. ## Prototype behavior

| Decision | Implemented policy |
| --- | --- |
| Posture | Use the committed threat and friendly strength, morale, native ratios and restrictions. Separate entry and exit thresholds and a five-second commitment stop a unit cycling between flight and advance. The cover multiplier changes from 1.5 in cover to 1.35 for four seconds after leaving it. Morale below 30 or threat above twice the cover-adjusted retreat threshold breaks the five-second dwell immediately when retreat is permitted. A healthy, outmatched soldier leaving the stock rally branch holds in cover or falls back in the open. |
| Squad movement | At least two eligible humans and one eligible contact activate the squad. The coordinator aggregates strength, morale, contact positions and recently measured lines of fire. It commits to hold, advance, flank or fallback for six seconds. A committed flank uses a 45° offset and a six-point margin to change sides. Medics and heavy-weapon carriers provide overwatch. Assault carriers and riflemen alternate in roster order, with at most four issued bounds per phase. The phase changes after seven seconds or after the moving element holds at its destinations for 3.5 seconds. Each cover bound requires cover occupancy within 2 m of its goal. An open-ground bound uses a 2.5 m arrival radius. Larger rosters rotate. |
| Firing and movement | Fighters can keep an open shot within 78% of selected-weapon range, or 65% for an SMG. Returning false from the movement decision lets the enclosing state continue to its native attack. The native search uses a 6 m radius around the intended goal. Advancing soldiers accept cover 5–18 m from their position and withdrawing soldiers accept cover 8–20 m away. If cover is unavailable, a rejoining soldier, an exposed withdrawing soldier or an advancing soldier without a shot to hold can issue a native point move. The point must satisfy cohesion, 2.5 m spacing, dry ground and the corridor check. The 28 m squad and 18 m overwatch limits restrict destinations and permit an already-separated soldier to close the gap. The native follower replan now compares squared displacement with a squared 2 m threshold, or 6 m in squad combat. |
| Cover choice and withdrawal | Keep native collection, path rejection, geometry, occupancy and score. Re-rank its first 12 descriptors using at most six segment probes. An open line of fire during advance or a blocked line during withdrawal adds 12. Screening by the cover edge adds another eight for a directed bound or retreat. A strong native candidate can win with neither bonus. Lateral peeks require a confirmed open shot. Flank alignment adds up to six. Crowding costs 15 within 2.5 m, tapering to zero at 3 m. Equal scores keep native order. Soft squad claims last 12 seconds. Ordinary cover updates require nine seconds of stability or an exposed position under fire or unable to shoot, and an eight-point improvement over the incumbent. Withdrawal weighs observed hostile Human AttackRate by distance, age and intent. Friendly direction contributes at most one quarter and only with a positive alignment above 0.25. |
| Blocked line of fire | With a four-second retry interval, first test whether a crouched or standing pose opens the shot. Otherwise evaluate paired lateral offsets at 2.5, 5 and 7.5 m. An endpoint needs dry, passable ground and a clear shot. Exposure, squad spacing and flank alignment break the choice. Prefer nearby validated cover, then a native point move. An unavailable segment result supplies no evidence of either a shot or defilade. |
| Cover injury and idle fallback | Retain cover after a distant hit if the current threat is screened. A second visible, attacking or threatening enemy on an unscreened side restores the native injury decision. AI-controlled infantry in cover, recently engaged, or with a committed hostile observation within 60 m clears the idle return flag before stock fallback. Player-owned idle anchors stay under player control. |

Native event `0x10` reaches battle state slot 5 at `0x96B334`. The two slot bodies own the tactical deadline at `state+0x3EC`. Outdoors, advance and assault rearm for 4 s, fallback for 3 s and break contact for 2.5 s exposed or 5 s screened. In cover the corresponding values are 5.5 s, 5 s, and 2 s exposed or 4.5 s screened. Hold uses the same firing dwell as advance. The module also records a private firing dwell after completion. [Offline fixtures](../tests/test_tactical_infantry_26734796.cpp)

## Where the hooks run

Human brain slot 35, `0x95A680`, calls the target refresh at `0x95A699`. On return at `0x95A69E`, the subject at `brain+0x3B8` is current. The refresh adapter can now prevent the native timed rally from sampling the engine RNG. After stock posture selection, the call at `0x95ABD6` enters `0x91C350` with return address `0x95ABDB`. The dispatch adapter computes individual posture, then applies the current squad assignment. During advance, bounders and lagging members may advance and covering members hold. A holding squad can send a laggard to rejoin. Fallback caps posture at withdrawal when retreat is permitted. This reaches the native movement gate at `0x969430`, which skips a soldier in posture 2 without a direct goal. The adapter commits the final `int32` posture at `+0x52C` and clears the `uint32` deadline at `+0x530` before stock state dispatch. Both adapters check their caller. Stock target ownership and facing updates remain on their original schedule.

The squad producer at `0x949450` and its destination writer at `0x94BDB0` still update the native roster and waypoints. The module handles the follow signal in the individual battle state. In cover it clears bit 9 through the native setter before the unconditional exit. Outdoors it admits the follower to the movement branch at `0x963422` and suppresses the redundant commander-follow order during combat. Actual aiming, screening and offensive movement use the acting soldier’s own eligible current observation. An ally’s observation contributes to the squad centroid without granting the soldier visibility. [Target and visibility contract](target-prioritization-26734796.md)

## Verification and limits

The collision audit found **15 distinct tactical entries and 46 distinct existing entries**, with zero overlaps. For each tactical hook, an offline MinHook trampoline fixture copied the original PE bytes, checked complete displaced instructions covering at least five bytes, and verified the relay and continuation address. The prototype passes `enable_prototype = true` after the fingerprint check and enables all staged hooks with one MinHook call. Passing false during staging keeps the tactical adapters passive. Partial tactical staging removes prior entries in reverse order. The exported hot-unload entry, `ShutdownAndUnload`, closes admission and drains in-flight adapters before freeing their trampolines. It snapshots the total issued tactical orders before resetting module state and reports that count as `tactOrd`. Scene changes and clock rewinds reset the 1,024 soldier slots, 128 squads and 256 claims.

The existing Move-at-Will, targeting and support exports supplied most entries. One focused Ghidra export supplied 13 further functions. The compiler’s 19-byte leaf comparator had no Ghidra function record and was checked byte-for-byte in the PE. The combined evidence preserves source export hashes and exact disassembly. It covers 60 method entries, 812 instruction locations, seven RTTI tables, three constants and 30 byte sequences. There are 59 exact candidate records. The existing squad Quant export contained an interior request, so its enclosing function was matched to the PE unwind entry at `0x949450`.
