## Authority and ownership

Both entry points require the shared host serial actor scope, matching concrete brain and live identity. Inhibition, Direct Control, dispatch block (bit 20), inside-Exec (bit 24), firing, aiming, reloading and higher coordinator priorities veto intervention. Hold Position and active player move orders (bit 28) are admitted only while an ordinary move is already active (idle Hold Position units are never moved), always resume the exact original destination, and immediately abort if the player replaces the order. The convoy also yields to repair episodes and reversing vehicles. Slot `brain+0xF0` must contain an owner-bound move or a sequence with one such move. An Attack, cover or support parent, queued tail, foreign watched task or external order listener preserves the existing order.

The last XYZ in the `eMove+0x40..48` vector or the verified point task (`eMoveTask+0x40`, vtable `0x1108C90`, XY at `+0x10`) supplies the destination. The adapters exclude coordinates that trip the point factory's zero-coordinate diagnostic. Hull forward comes from `chassis+0x5C`, with actor orientation and travel direction as fallbacks. Velocity `0x73A2A0` is converted using the world's time and length scales.

The shared `ai_support::move` installer requires a free slot. Stream D builds the complete native sequence privately using constructor `0x918560`, point factory `0x90A8C0`, wait factory `0x8E2710` and append `0x919270`. After revalidating the source and actor, brain slot 13 takes ownership. The shared Exec and destructor hooks guard each point task by generation. Native update `0x9189B0` advances after a child finishes, including failure. Early release calls `0x9193F0(sequence, 1)` to retain only the return task in the still-owned sequence. A changed order, missing watch, scene change or clock rewind revokes the detour. A vacant slot never triggers restoration. The saved move returns to stock ownership when its Exec is admitted.
