Steam build `26734796`, `call_to_arms.exe`, SHA-256 `81a13de787c1869b766004cdcadb9ff99012f0b1b58b89db9a7741b10b9f059a`. **runtime_allowed: false**. The evidence below is static game assembly and offline adapter tests. Addresses are game RVAs and field offsets are hexadecimal.

## Authority and integration

Eight new detours bring the infantry translation unit to 42 hooks and the unified DLL to 71. The four existing hooks in the table retain their original trampolines. The reload tick uses the existing post-quant callback. Every added decision requires an accepted fingerprint, an open lifecycle gate, the owner’s serial actor update, a local simulation, and a matching concrete brain. Owner identity, chassis, weaponry and inhibition are checked at the point of use. Shutdown closes the gate, drains inflight callbacks, removes hooks and releases cooldown storage. Partial staging unwinds in reverse order.

## Verification

The eight prologue lengths, in table order with both stance hooks and the two order/target hooks, are 5, 7, 5, 5, 5, 5, 6 and 5 bytes. Each spans whole instructions with no relative branch or RIP-relative operand. The hit-field loads and float constants for the boarding and target-memory gates were checked against the fingerprinted PE. The stock query, movement, event and reload paths own their normal simulation output. The fixes use deterministic geometry and private ray buffers.

The offline suite exercises native fallbacks, synthetic holder and order ownership, scene and cooldown changes, target memory, blocking geometry, failure returns, partial hook rollback and authority gates. The runtime checks still pending include single-player and private host/client observation of movement, kit retention, inventory changes, window events, stance and ammo updates on this exact executable.
