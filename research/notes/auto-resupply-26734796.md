## When a unit travels

- Admit a concrete AI or player-owned (`control` `0`/`1`) human or vehicle brain on the host’s serial actor phase (`!0xA7A80() && !0xC2B7A0()`). Respect Direct Control, inhibition, dispatch block bit 20, active player and saved/default orders, and Hold Position (`move_mode == 1`; bit 24 vetoes new-order installation in `free_slot()` only). An occupied order, including a grenade dodge, takes precedence. Defer injured or non-operative infantry, supply-zone carriers, occupied inventory managers, units with no supported primary and damaged vehicle bodies.
- For infantry, inspect actual right-hand and box items. Prefer a firearm over a pistol or revolver, with the held gun winning ties within a class. For a vehicle or gun, inspect real non-dummy mounted weapons with a charger and use the largest supported finite calibre. Require a compatible ammunition selector. An empty magazine alone cannot trigger a trip. Positive loaded ammunition, a matching reserve stack, or a second loaded copy of the same primary prevents departure. A matching human supply row requires an inventory weapon searchable by the stock box worker. A vehicle must have a native fill or a matching item in an already resident raw template that establishes positive capacity. The policy does not call the lazy template loader.
- Rank friendly sources by 3D distance, up to 60 m, with entity ID breaking exact ties. Check the zone’s current enabled bit, positive radius and period, a matching affordable row, native recipient capacity, health and ownership. Reject a burning or destroyed truck, disallowed source chassis state, foreign team restriction, contested point or unproven flag ownership. Unowned static supply crates (`crates_set02_resupply`, excluding orphaned `flagpoint_ammo` and structure-bone children) are eligible when serviceable and unthreatened. Loose ammunition outside a live supply zone is ineligible.
- Reject visible enemies, hostile sightings from the previous 5 s, or recent incoming fire on an observer near either the source or endpoint within 25 m (scanning committed sensors of all allied actors, without falsely vetoing safe unguarded rear flags or crates that have no operatable actor parked beside them). Read only committed sensor data. [Sensor observations](auto-smoke-26734796.md)
- Choose a dry, accessible offset within the live stock radius, with alternate angles, short detours and spacing from both nearby actors and other active resupply endpoints. An actor already inside waits in place. Allow up to four concurrent inbound trips per source until refill, failure or timeout. The trip releases on a positive primary ammo observation. Abandon it on a changed weapon or owner, unsafe source, player intervention, loss of the destination or nine seconds without progress. Travel is capped at 40 s and 60 m of accumulated 3D movement, including detours. Inside the zone, allow two native refill periods plus four seconds. Failed trips cool down for 20 s and successful ones for 30 s. A failed unit/source pair is also skipped for 60 s, allowing another safe source to win. The module never orders a return trip.

The bounded tables support 256 concurrent trips/cooldowns. Positive-ammo units consume only a scan timestamp. Unmatched, exhausted or unobserved sources are reconsidered after six seconds. Native movement issues the checked point via `0x90A8C0`. Its `eMoveTask` creates the child `eMove` that emits command 0x09 when the host sync flag is set. The stock supply worker alone performs refill and spends the source budget. ### Capture flags in multiplayer and skirmish

The stock multiplayer and campaign flag structure definitions declare the place `"ammo" "flagpoint_ammo"`. The child is a game entity using `flag_ammo_heavy` from `properties/resupply.inc`. Its base definition sets a 15 m radius, a 10 s resupply period, a 10 s regeneration period and a 500-point limit. The campaign capture-the-flag override disables regeneration and keeps the refill period. Definitions can vary by mode, so the policy reads each live extender. The native radius, item, budget and period checks belong to the same non-client stock supply worker. A mission must actually instantiate the child or attach a supply zone for the flag to resupply. [Read-only stock member fingerprints](../artifacts/build-26734796/auto-resupply-26734796.json)

The stock German `blitz3_6_art_ammo` truck selects `truck_ammo_heavy`. The `crates_set02_resupply` wooden crate selects `supply_ammo_fixed` with 250 supply and declares `burning` at rest. The module reads the live burning extender and damage state to distinguish an intact source from one on fire. Both stock members are fingerprinted in the fine map.

The structure owner has a placer at +0x568. Its 0x298-byte place records contain the live child at +0x280. A structure bone, when present, also names the owner at +0x88. Match the actual child pointer in the parent’s records. A colocated crate cannot establish the relationship. A direct zone on a map point goes through the same capture test. At map-point extender+0xB58, require the exact capture engine and its back-reference. Require a matching current team, progress 1, delta 0, allied flag owner and no positive capture weight for any other team in the ten floats beginning at +0x108. These are stock update inputs at `0xD1B720` and `0xD1D050`. The additional sensor veto covers a known nearby enemy even when outstanding capture progress is still full. ## Host integration and verification

Include `mods/auto_resupply_module.hpp`. Create the shared disabled hooks once with `goh::ai_support::prepare_hooks(base, measured_sha256, stock_content, create, remove)`. Enter `ai_support::ActorQuantScope` around the existing serial actor slot 23 at `0x804AC0`. After the original returns, pass the reacquired live actor to `auto_resupply::tick` inside the same scope. If both modules are enabled, tick Auto-Medic first. Resupply can operate independently with its own shared actor scope and the stock supply worker. The sibling DLL integration file is a separate integration step. Shutdown requires quiescent owned orders before removing the hooks. [Complete integration and teardown sequence](auto-medic-26734796.md#host-integration)

For a fresh focused export, derive the candidate TSV from `.methods[] | [.entry_rvas.game,.id] | @tsv` in the map. Use `run_headless.py --build-id 26734796 export --focused --program call_to_arms.exe` with that TSV and output `local-work/exports/build-26734796/call_to_arms-auto-resupply`. Verify with `research/scripts/verify_fine_map.py` and the installed binary/resource directories. The offline fixtures cover team weights, capacity-cache misses, flag-child identity, loaded ammunition, budgets, source claims, cooldowns, preemption, smoke and incoming fire.
