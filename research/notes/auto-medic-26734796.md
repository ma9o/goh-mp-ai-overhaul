## Choosing and treating a patient

| Decision | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Admit a medic | Require the concrete host human brain, an operative living human, native medic ability and veterancy predicate `0x96AAF0(root)`, AI or player-owned control (`0`/`1`), Free or Zone movement (`0`/`2`; rejects Hold Position `1`), and Open or Return Fire. The brain inhibition counter, dispatch inhibitor bit 20, active player order bit 28, Direct Control chassis/weaponry signals, occupied default or saved orders, and an occupied current order all veto admission. Bit 24 (inside current-order callback) vetoes `free_slot()` new-order installation only, never `permit()` revalidation during `Exec`. An empty, idle native sequence can accept one child. A current grenade dodge retains the actor. |
| Claim a patient | Rank allied scene members within 36 m by 3D distance, breaking ties by entity ID. Require a live vitality receiver, the stock revivable predicate `0x379260`, an open treatment lock at patient+0x12BA, and a reachable approach. Skip a patient claimed by this module or already targeted by another current native Medic. One job owns each patient through its cooldown. Actor identity is checked again against current scene membership. |
| Find smoke | Check the medic’s right hand and box with the existing positive smoke-key filter. A donor must be an idle, non-DC ally within 24 m (passive donors on Hold Position/Hold Fire remain eligible since only the medic moves). A vehicle donor also needs a live crew list, an operative hull and an available chassis. Prefer stacks of at least two audited grenades; non-medic donors may give a single audited grenade, while another medic donor must retain at least one. The medic approaches within 2 m and checks a ground corridor from both units to their meeting point. Only the medic moves. Both modules respect the donor reservation. |
| Screen | Choose a point toward a visible or recently observed hostile, falling back to the patient’s location when there is no threat direction or the threat ray is obstructed/too close. Move closer once if the point is beyond throw range. Use stock WeaponShot, Fire and the audited true-smoke send/spawn path. Check the carrier, exact held definition, point, trajectory, reservation, authority and patient again at release (supporting animation-marker releases outside `ActorQuantScope`). |
| Confirm cover | At release, record the existing stock smoke cylinders within 7 m of the intended screen. After the paired packet and local spawn, wait up to 12 s for a newly observed cylinder within 7 m (accounting for grenade bounce/roll during the 3 s fuse). An unrelated pre-existing cylinder or the mere disappearance of the grenade cannot advance the job. |
| Revive or defer | Bind a fresh stock Medic to the claimed patient after the smoke order finishes. Recheck cover direction (5 m lateral plume margin), patient displacement (2 m), the live volume and treatment ownership during the order. If no eligible donor exists, allow unscreened treatment when no committed sighting or incoming-fire evidence appears around the patient within 25 m. Recheck that condition during treatment. |

A job allows 14 s to fetch, 8 s for exchange, 16 s for the approach, 9 s for the shot, and 35 s for treatment, under a 70 s total deadline. A failed search retries after 2 s. Completed or abandoned assignments cool down for 12 s. The table holds 128 medics. A full table defers new work. Death, prior revival, ownership changes, player control, a replaced order or a scene rewind withdraws authorization. [Target and smoke contracts](auto-smoke-26734796.md)

## Transfer, throw and revive boundaries

1. `0x7E1D50` builds the donor’s source location with its actual box, cell bytes and an explicit amount of one. `0x7E1F40` must resolve that location to the selected item. `0x7E1CE0` builds the receiving box location. The stock `Xchange` builder at `0x7DF910` takes the identity snapshot in `0x7DFEE0`. `0x7DFBF0` submits it to the source owner’s manager. Successful submission yields an action ID and emits `eInventoryTransferCommand` (0x10) on the emitting host. The operation constructor copies the requested quantity at `0x7E6E67`. Get-quantity at `0x7EDE60` uses that amount when no explicit parameter overrides it. The peer enqueues the decoded operation at `0x3E1E20`. The module leaves splitting, fit, placement and consumption to the native operation.
2. The stock manager checks ordering and its exclusive token before calling `0x7E66C0` at `0x7DDFE9`. The host and vanilla peer advance their operation through the same native schedule. On the serial actor update, a changed queue, donor, patient or authority cancels the matching pending action through `0x7DDDC0` and `0x7E6A40` (command 0x11). The manager retains its normal destruction and compaction path. A destructor mark, operation generation and action ID protect against address reuse. The next stage requires a positive matching item in the medic’s own inventory after the native operation has drained. [Inventory ownership](inventory-ownership-map-25492057.md) · [Equipment actions](equipment-actions-map-25492057.md)
3. The medic shares Auto-Smoke’s marked-order tracking, exact eight positive stock keys and trajectory checks. WP, gas and supply-marker variants fail the positive key check. Two creation-time detour adapters extend its WeaponShot and release checks to the claimed patient, including patients outside the squad. Ordinary throws use the existing trampolines. The stock event script creates `smoke_volume_cylinder` or `smoke_volume_cylinder_sm`. Their live presence is a cover proxy. Actual line-of-sight occlusion at the patient still needs a private-lobby check. [Smoke implementation and stock content evidence](auto-smoke-26734796.md)
4. `0x378670(patient)` builds `Order::eMedic` with a linked patient receiver. `0x378710` reads the host role, emits `eOrderMedicCommand` (0x58) at its treatment transitions and owns the native healing lock. The command applies on peers at `0xAF1D0`. `0x379910` performs native completion. Custom code observes the lock, order, inventory and vitality and writes none of those fields.

The shared movement probe initializes the actor’s path context at `0x8DF010`. It clamps and checks short dry-ground segments with `0x8C19E0`. Every segment is strictly shorter than the verified 16-world-unit divisor at `0x10F7100`. At `0x8C2070`, that gives one endpoint sample and skips the interior-sample RNG draw. The search tries six endpoint angles and then four side waypoints, 3 m or 7 m to either side of the route midpoint. Longer routes around obstacles can be deferred. An accepted move uses the normal point task, path search and replicating child Move. Native actions retain their ordinary engine behavior. [Movement and replication](lof-detour-soft-cover-26734796.md)

## Host integration

The module is supplied as a header. The integration owner can use the following sequence after runtime approval for this exact build.

1. Include `mods/auto_medic_module.hpp`. Measure the on-disk PE hash and establish that the audited stock definitions are loaded. In the existing disabled-hook batch, call `goh::ai_support::prepare_hooks(base, digest, stock, create, remove)` once for both support modules. It registers the two owned order Exec guards and their concrete deleting destructors.
2. At the existing Auto-Smoke installation site, use `goh::auto_medic::prepare_smoke_hooks(base, digest, stock, create, remove)`. It registers the concrete transfer destructor observer and delegates the existing smoke batch, substituting its two adapters at creation time. Each installer rolls back its own creations on failure. The integration owner must roll back earlier successful batches if a later batch fails.
3. In the verified serial `Hook_ActorSlot23` at `0x804AC0`, construct `goh::ai_support::ActorQuantScope support_scope(actor)` alongside the existing Auto-Smoke scope before the original call. Save `ai_support::identity(actor)` before calling the original for a live actor. After it returns, within both scopes, reacquire the actor with `ai_support::resolve(world, saved_identity)` and pass a successful result to `auto_medic::tick`, followed by `auto_resupply::tick` if enabled. This gives native sensor commit, due grenade dodge and player orders first opportunity. The shared guard requires the serial thread, actor scope, non-worker `0xA7A80`, and non-client `0xC2B7A0` (active in SP/Skirmish/Conquest and MP host; passive on MP clients/workers, with native wire senders gating on `0xC2B740`).
4. Stop admitting work at mission teardown. Let native mission destruction drain marked orders, transfers and smoke intents with guards still present. Require both `ai_support::quiescent()` and `auto_medic::quiescent()` before removing hooks and joining callbacks. Then call `auto_resupply::reset()` if used, `auto_medic::after_hooks_removed()` (also releases Auto-Smoke’s TLS) and `ai_support::after_hooks_removed()`. Keep the DLL loaded while native callbacks can refer to it.
