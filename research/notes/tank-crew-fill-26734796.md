# Vehicle crew redistribution and passenger refill on Steam build 26734796

**Static implementation for the x64 game executable.** The module admits SHA-256 `81a13de787c1869b766004cdcadb9ff99012f0b1b58b89db9a7741b10b9f059a` and installs zero additional MinHook detours (`hook_count = 0`), running from the shared serial `eActor::slot23` post-quant callback immediately after Tank Auto-Repair. ## Scope and selection policy

Stock `ePlacer::Plan` (`0x7954A0`) searches within the requested seat group. An unavailable seat can abort a multi-seat request and leave the temporary donor flag (bit 13) set on occupied crew seats. The module considers each vacancy separately and moves one occupant through the normal boarder. It leaves the stock planner and its flags alone.

| Aspect | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Eligible vehicles** | Upright (within 70°), unlinked vehicle/cannon actors with a routed, concrete `Actor::eVehicleBrain` (`0x101FD68`) and their own canonical root boarder. Burning, sinking, destroyed, exploded or abandoned actors are excluded. The component vector at `actor+0xB90` must contain valid owner-bound rows (stride `0x28`). A `body` row, when present, must be working. Nonzero engine, track or wheel state marks the vehicle immobilized. |
| **Passenger sources** | Occupied `passenger` seats with no visor and Attach, registered weapon/chassis request and NoCollect clear (`(flags & 0x9200) == 0`). A tank class or definition tagged `tank` with tracks requires a named, resolved LinkBone, as on hull seats. Other vehicles also admit cabin seats with an empty LinkBone name. A named but unresolved bone disqualifies the seat on every vehicle. Roof MG and passenger-group APC duty seats remain excluded by their registered request or visor. |
| **Internal sources** | Occupied `crew` seats with Attach and NoCollect clear (`(flags & 0x8200) == 0`) that are not currently servicing a satisfied native `ePlacer` request in `placer+0x50..+0x58` (e.g. a cannon's active `charger` reload request). Registered crew requests and the stock temporary donor flag are allowed when idle. Crew and passengers share the live human, authority, ownership, walker, reservation and kind-2 source-node checks. |
| **Eligible destinations (freed crew slots)** | Empty, unreserved, enabled, unsealed `group == "crew"` slots passing the native `ePlacer::IsPlaceAvailable` (`0x79C2F0`) predicates and resolving to a kind-2 Place node in `eBoarder::FindNode` (`0x79E930`). Stock `tank-main.inc` disables a killed crewman's seat (`flags & 0x10`) for 8 seconds before `{crew_enable place ... 1}`, which the module honors automatically. |
| **Crew slot fill order** | `gunner` → `driver` → `charger` → `commander` → secondary cannon gunners (`>= 37 mm` via `place+0x5E0`) → secondary chargers (`charger1`..`charger6`) → other registered crew/bow-MG slots (`flags & 0x1000`) → remaining `"crew"` slots, breaking ties by slot index. |
| **Passenger priority** | Eligible passengers and donors tied on role rank in this order from highest to lowest. (0) dedicated tank crewmen (`"crew"` tag at `breed+0x440` + Mechanic ability `0x107F0F8`), (1) skilled mechanics without `"crew"` tag (`rate >= 0.99`, e.g. `tankman_ace` and singleplayer `tankman`), (2) other `"crew"`-tagged personnel, (3) basic mechanics (`driver` breeds), (4) ordinary infantry, (5) infantry specialists (Medics via `0x107EEA8` and AT-weapon tags `soldier_bazooka`/`soldier_pzscheck`/`soldier_pzfaust`/`soldier_atr`/`soldier_atr_grenade`). Ties break by same-player ownership, mechanic rate, slot index, and entity ID. |

### Selection within one vehicle update

1. Fill the highest-priority available destination. For an internal promotion, pull from the least critical occupied role first, breaking equivalent-role ties by passenger rank. A bow gunner can take a vacant main gun before the commander does. Ordinary promotions must improve role priority and may fill roles through secondary loader. They preserve the number of vacant crew seats, including when a repairer is due back.
2. If there is no ordinary promotion, try the best eligible passenger. This transfer consumes one vacancy and requires more free crew seats than expected returnees. On the next update a passenger can backfill a seat freed by an internal promotion.
3. With no eligible passenger, a driver may take the main gun while free of movement orders if immobilized or facing a visible candidate. A gunner may take the wheel only as the sole occupied crewman, on a movement order, with working mobility. A successful swap in either direction starts a shared three-second simulation-time cooldown for that vehicle. An eligible passenger waiting for a repairer's seat still suppresses both emergency swaps.

The `[modules]` switches `tank_crew_fill`, `internal_crew_swap`, and `nontank_crew_fill` default to true. The first controls the whole module. The second enables all crew-to-crew moves, including emergency swaps. The third admits non-tanks for both crew and passenger moves. The shutdown summary reports successful moves in total and by breed tier, plus overlapping counts for internal moves and moves on non-tanks.

## Native boundary and multiplayer replication

All addresses are build `26734796` game RVAs (relocated from the `25492057` [placement](placement-map-25492057.md), [boarding](boarding-map-25492057.md), and [crew actions](crew-actions-map-25492057.md) maps).

| Native boundary | Build `26734796` RVA | Contract used by module |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Entity::eCannonExtender` | `0x110F040` vtable, state setter `0x942170` | Startup verifies primary RTTI `.?AVeCannonExtender@Entity@@`. An entry in the actor's extender list must match the vtable and owner back-pointer (`ext+0x18`) to identify its cannon. Only fire state (`int(ext+0xC30) == 0`) admits a move. Move (1), trailing (2), and other nonzero states suppress both transfer kinds. A parent link (`actor+0x1C0` bit 28) independently excludes the vehicle. |
| Vehicle movement and visibility | `Actor::eVehicleBrain` and `Order::eMoveTask` (`0x1023970`) | Movement intent is any of brain `+0x39C` bits 6, 9 or 28, or an owner-bound move task on the active order chain. The candidate vector at brain `+0x3A0..+0x3A8` contains subject pointers (8-byte stride). A non-null candidate with bit 29 set at subject `+0x10` supplies current visibility for the driver decision. These are read-only inputs. |
| `eBoarder::Move` | `0x7A0240` | Direct seat transfer (same path invoked by the vehicle crew UI at `0xB88BC5`). Emits source occupancy via `0x7905B0`, reserves destination via `0x7987F0`, resolves both Place nodes via `0x79E930`, invokes `StartWalker` (`0x7A1740`) → `AdvanceWalker` (`0x7A1FA0`), emits owner event `0x19`, and on MP hosts (`0xC2B740`) publishes native wire command **`0x35` (`eBoarderMoveCommand`, `0x161C10`/`0x161C50`/`0x161CC0`)**. On standard tanks where hull passenger doors (`emit3+`) do not connect to interior crew hatches (`emit0..2`), `FindNextHop` (`0x79E970`) returns null and `AdvanceWalker` synchronously calls `Finish` (`0x7A4DA0`) → `Collect` (`0x7988B0`) → `Include` (`0x78F2D0`), without requiring the tank to stop. |
| `eBoarder::Root` / `FindNode` | `0x79D790` / `0x79E930` | Verifies the tank is its own canonical root boarder and that both source and destination slots have valid kind-2 runtime Place nodes before invoking `0x7A0240`. |
| `ePlacer::IsPlaceAvailable` | `0x79C2F0` | Layout contract mirrored directly in policy (`!(flags & 0x10)`, resolved bone if `linkBone` name is non-empty, `occupant == 0`, `reserved == 0`, `!(flags & 0x100000)`). |
| `ePlacer::Request` / `Release` / `SatisfyRequest` | `0x7945A0` / `0x794780` / `0x795290` (`0x7954A0`, `0x795830`) | Active native crew requests live in `placer+0x50..+0x58` (stride `0x348`), with requested `eName` entries at `req+0x318..+0x320` (stride `0x60`, string at `+0x40`) and a `satisfied` byte at `req+0x341`. While satisfied (such as `charger_Reload` at `0x8213F0` holding `"charger"`), `SatisfyRequest` immediately pulls a free crewman back via `ePlacerMoveCommand` (`0x39`), so the module skips any occupied interior seat named by a live satisfied request until stock releases it. |
| `eActor::CanAccess` / `IsOperatable` | `0x80F710` / `0x8067E0` | Guards non-same-player allied passengers: allied transfers are rejected whenever the tank has no same-player crewman already seated (preventing `Finish` at `0x7A4DA0` from flipping tank player ownership when `Operatable` transiently clears) or when `CanAccess` returns false. |
| `Human::eBreed` tags & abilities | `0x971B40` / `0x1BA490` / `0x977AC0` | Reads breed `{tags ...}` from `breed+0x440..+0x448` (stride `0x60`) and ability infos from `breed+0x528+0x20` (`Human::eInfo_Mechanic` `0x107F0F8`, `Human::eInfo_Medic` `0x107EEA8`). |

Before invoking `0x7A0240`, the module also verifies the donor holds no stale reservation (`slot+0x290`) on any slot of the tank so `PickReservedPlace` (`0x7989F0`) inside `Collect` always resolves the exact target slot. Each vehicle issues at most one move per simulation tick. An accepted native call gives the move 1.5 seconds to settle. A rejected call gives it one second. Up to four recent `(human, src, dst)` attempts per vehicle retain independent escalating retry delays (`5 s` → `10 s` → `25 s`), so multiple crewmen on the same vehicle cannot erase each other's cooldowns. An empty source seat during transit preserves the retry history while its timer runs. An occupied destination confirms that the vacancy has closed.

## Authority, Direct Control, and repair/emit coordination

- **Host-only, serial main thread:** Guards on `!Core::IsQuantWorkerThread()` (`0xA7A80`) and `!IsRemoteClient()` (`0xC2B7A0`), staying active in SP/Skirmish/Conquest and on MP hosts while remaining completely passive on remote MP clients. Zero custom engine RNG draws.
- **Direct Control & manual orders:** Bails out on vehicle Direct Control (`chassis+0x18 != 0`), manual weaponry (`weaponry+0x70` bit 1), or any vehicle brain inhibition (`(brain+0x39C & 0x3F) != 0`). Accepts AI (`control == 0`) and player-owned (`control == 1`) vehicles across `Move Free`, `Hold Position`, and `Move in Zone` because interior seat transfer does not displace the tank. Seated donors pass `available_seat` (rejecting Direct Control, manual order bit 28, incapacitation, or inhibition counts beyond the single native seat-link inhibition with flag 19).
- **Repair and boarding de-confliction:** Counts live conscious returning crew from `tank_auto_repair::collect_returning_crew` deduplicated with scene-wide active `Order::eSmartRepair` (`0x10225B0`), `Order::eRepair` (`0x110D0E8`), `Order::eKitExchange` (`0x10224F8`), and `Order::eBoard` (`0x110B590`) orders targeting the tank. Passenger fills proceed only when `available_crew_slots > expected_returning_crew`, preserving room for a returning crewman while still refilling genuinely vacated slots (including if an outside repairer is killed).
- **Disembark protection:** Defers whenever `placer+0x48 != 0`, any exit walker (`final_node->kind == 1`) is active, or `Order::eEmit` (`0x110B808`) is active on the tank. A non-passenger `Order::eEmit` (`mode != 1`) starts a 10 s per-tank backoff so the module does not fight manual crew disembarkation.
