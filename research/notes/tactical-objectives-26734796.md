## Integration and verification

After the executable fingerprint matches and all hooks activate, `InitThread` calls `reset()` to open objective admission and zero the counters. Both initialization rollback paths clear objective state. On the host’s serial `Hook_ActorSlot23` post-quant path, the DLL resolves the live actor, ticks Auto-Medic and Auto-Resupply, then passes `close_grenade::is_busy(supportWorld, liveActor)` into the objective tick. This gives grenade actions and support assignments priority. [Unified entry point](../../mods/goh_ai_mod.cpp)

`ShutdownAndUnload` closes objective admission before draining grenade work and shared watched orders. After the hooks and callbacks drain, it snapshots the issued-move counters, clears objective state and logs `objPursue` and `objCap`. DLL process detach also closes admission.
